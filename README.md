# TNMT Retail Sales Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that explores product revenue performance in retail sales data.

**Tools used:** Excel (data cleaning, formulas, PivotTables) and Power BI (modelling and dashboards)

**Data:** Retail sales dataset provided through a training programme.

## Objectives
- Analyse product revenue performance.
- Identify the top 10 and bottom 10 products by revenue.
- Compare revenue across product categories.
- Compare average revenue by product name.

## What I built
- Used Excel to clean and prepare the data, create formulas and calculated columns, build PivotTables and summaries, and check the data before loading it into Power BI.
- Horizontal bar charts for the top 10 and bottom 10 products by revenue.
- A bar chart of total revenue by product category.
- A visual of average revenue by product name.
- Filters for product, category and date.

## Key findings
- [Top product(s) by revenue and the amount]
- [Lowest-performing product(s) or category]
- [Which category contributed the most revenue]
